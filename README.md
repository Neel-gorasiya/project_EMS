# project_EMS
EMS Pro — A modern Employee Management System with dashboard, staff directory, task tracking (Kanban &amp; list), leave management, shift logs, department overview, and analytics. Dark mode, live search &amp; employee profiles included.


🌟 What is EMS Pro?
EMS Pro (Employee Management System) is a modern, web-based application designed to help businesses and organizations manage their entire workforce digitally — all from a single, easy-to-use dashboard. It replaces paper registers, Excel sheets, and scattered WhatsApp messages with a clean, professional system.

⚙️ How It Works — Page by Page
1. 🏠 Dashboard
The central command center of the app.

Shows live statistics — total employees, active shifts, pending tasks, leave requests
Has a real-time shift timer — click "Clock In" to start your shift timer, "Clock Out" to stop it
Shows current shift details — your location, role, and reporting supervisor
Displays recent activity feed — who clocked in, what tasks were completed, new leave requests
Has an Employee Overview table — quick snapshot of your team's status
How it helps: A manager opens the app in the morning and instantly knows: how many people are working today, what tasks are overdue, and if anyone requested leave — without asking anyone.

2. 👥 Employee Directory
A complete digital employee database.

Lists every employee with their photo initials, department, role, hire date, and status
Search & filter by name, email, department, role, or status (Active / On Leave / Inactive)
Add new employees with full details — name, email, phone, department, role, position, hire date, salary, supervisor
Edit any employee's details anytime
Delete employees who have left
View Profile — opens a detailed side panel showing the employee's tasks, leave history, and shifts
Export to CSV — download the full employee list as an Excel-compatible file
How it helps: HR managers can onboard new employees in seconds and always have updated records. No more searching through files or Excel sheets.

3. ✅ Task Management
A complete work tracking system with two views:

📋 List View — traditional table showing all tasks with:

Task title, assigned employee, due date, priority (High/Medium/Low), status
📌 Kanban Board — visual card-based view with 3 columns:

Pending → In Progress → Completed
Features:

Create new tasks and assign them to any employee
Filter tasks by status, priority, or assignee
Edit task details or delete tasks
Priority badges (High = red, Medium = orange, Low = green)
How it helps: Managers can see at a glance what work is pending, what's being done, and what's completed — exactly like Trello or Jira but built into their HR system.

4. 🗓️ Leave Management
Handles all employee leave requests in one place.

Shows summary counts — Pending, Approved, Rejected requests
Employees can submit leave requests with type (Annual/Sick/Personal/Unpaid), dates, and reason
Managers can Approve ✓ or Reject ✗ requests with one click
Auto-calculates number of days
History of all past leave requests visible
How it helps: No more WhatsApp messages asking "can I take leave?" — everything is formal, documented, and tracked.

5. ⏰ Shift Management
Tracks who worked when and where.

Logs clock-in time, clock-out time, location, and hours worked for every shift
Auto-calculates total hours worked per shift
Shows status — Active (still working) or Completed
Add new shift records manually
How it helps: Perfect for businesses with field staff, multiple shifts, or remote workers. Replaces manual attendance registers.

6. 🏢 Departments
Manages your organization structure.

View all departments as color-coded cards
See employee count per department
Know who the department head is
Add, edit, or delete departments anytime
How it helps: Keeps the organizational chart organized and automatically connects employees to their departments across the whole app.

7. 📊 Reports & Analytics
Gives data-driven insights about your workforce.

Attendance Rate — 94% (how regularly staff are showing up)
Task Completion Rate — 78% (how much work is getting done)
Average Hours/Day — 8.2 hrs
Employee Satisfaction Score — 4.7/5
Department Distribution Bar Chart — visual breakdown of employees per department
Task Status Donut Chart — shows Pending vs In Progress vs Completed tasks
How it helps: Business owners can see how productive their team is without manually counting anything.
